# G04 deep-research evaluation preregistration

Status: executed with preregistered early stop (2026-09-07). This document is a new
surface-boundary preregistration after the G04 research-round implementation.
Ling is a protocol/smoke subject only; Qwen 35B is the efficacy subject. This
does not authorize adoption, mirroring, or a defaults change.

## Question

Does the parent-owned evidence-gap round contract improve completion and
material citation support for complex, contested, multi-part research without
increasing unsupported claims, budget violations, or timeout-induced open
branches?

## Arms and subjects

The frozen comparison is the G03 representative baseline on the same admitted
research-shaped fixtures, with the same source cutoff, model, serving epoch,
and global budget. The treatment enables the dark hierarchical planner and
research-round profile. A Ketch-only arm is primary; Jina Reader fallback and
hybrid retrieval remain secondary, explicitly labelled arms if they are
available at execution time. The first efficacy subject is Qwen 35B
(`local-llamacpp/qwen36-35b-iq3s`). Ling
(`local-llamacpp/ling3-tiny-fast`) is run first as a model-neutral protocol
smoke and is not pooled into the Qwen efficacy decision.

## Measures and guards

The primary outcome is the preregistered completion score from the frozen G03
oracle. Secondary measures are material citation support, required-claim
coverage, explicit uncertainty, searches, discovery reads, parent validation
reads, latency, and timeout/open-branch rate. Hard guards are zero fabricated
or unverified final citations, zero cross-arm or cross-epoch evidence leaks,
no budget inflation, no malformed or orphan reports, and no blocked required
branch silently settling the head plan. A failure, no-go, or inconclusive
result is a valid outcome; it cannot change defaults or historical evidence.

## Cohort and procedure

Use the frozen G03 train/development/test manifests without modification and
issue a fresh source/config/surface receipt immediately before execution. This
screen uses the three admitted research-shaped cases (`research-comparative`,
`research-contested`, and `research-multi-part`) with one repetition and the
admitted fact-lookup negative control per subject. Run one bounded treatment
screen and the matched frozen comparison under identical case/seed/repetition
blocks, alternating arm order by case. Require parent rereads for every
citation used in the answer. Record the round ledger, graph state, safe
telemetry, and review packet; do not mirror, commit, or adopt a candidate.

## Stop rules

Stop on an invalid provenance binding, malformed report, evidence leak, budget
violation, timeout-induced open branch, or any hard guard failure. Stop after
the preregistered cohort and report a no-go/inconclusive result when the
minimum evidence threshold is not met. Do not expand search, delegation,
repetition, or model cohorts after seeing results.
