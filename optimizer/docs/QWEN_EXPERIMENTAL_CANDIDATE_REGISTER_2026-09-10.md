surfaces. It supersedes the premature “queue closed” conclusion in
`DARK_CANDIDATE_DISPOSITION_QWEN_2026-09-09.md` without changing any older
screen, receipt, or boundary row. A mechanism receipt proves that a configured
path ran; it does not prove task benefit. An absent screen is not a zero result.
