# G04 evidence — research driven by explicit evidence gaps

Status: implemented as a dark slice on 2026-09-07. An explicitly approved,
bounded Ling smoke and Qwen 35B comparison were run against a disposable
mirror; no live mirror, adoption, rollout, or default change was performed.

The parent-owned `pi.research-round/v1` ledger separates claim obligations and
evidence gaps from untrusted retrieved text. A typed round records selected
gaps, bounded query/source proposals, read receipts, parent evidence-card
metadata, conflicts, computed consumption, and a derived next action. The
ledger owns one global envelope (three searches, five discovery reads, and five
parent validation reads), reserves root child allocations before dispatch,
deduplicates retries and canonical URLs, charges failed children, and records
budget exhaustion as an explicit blocked gap. Child reports are accepted only
after a reservation and remain unverified; only a parent-validated,
non-truncated card can resolve a claim.

`research_plan_start` now creates this ledger and exposes the parent-only
`research_round` tool when `PLAN_GRAPH=on` and `DEEP_RESEARCH_PLANNING=on`.
The tool supports `start`, `record`, `inspect`, and `settle`; when explicit
rounds are recorded, `plan_settle` requires that research ledger to be settled.
Late child reports are merged once and cannot reopen a settled ledger. Telemetry records
safe counts only; URLs, queries, quotes, and page bodies are excluded.
